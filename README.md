# AVP Xenomorphs — RimWorld 1.6

Xenomorphs and Neomorphs from the Alien film franchise, ported from the original Alien vs Predator mod to RimWorld 1.6 as a Xenomorphs-only mod (no Yautja/USCM dependencies).

## Features

**Pawns:**
Queen, Drone (spitter), Warrior, Runner, Facehugger, Chestburster, Predalien, Thrumbomorph, Neomorph

**Mechanics:**
- Full Xenomorph life cycle: Egg → Facehugger → Chestburster → Adult
- Acidic Blood: damages everything in the same tile as living Xenomorph blood
- Hidden Infections: small chance any spawned pawn has a hidden impregnation (toggleable)
- Data-driven spawn system: Xenomorph type determined by host properties (XML-configurable)
- Facehugger mask: visible on infected pawns via native RimWorld render nodes
- Leap ability: facehuggers and runners can leap at targets
- EMP stun: Xenomorphs can be stunned by EMP weapons
- Cold stasis: Xenomorphs slow down in extreme cold (~-32F)

**Incidents:**
- They Mostly Come at Night: Xenomorph raids at night are larger
- Neomorph Spores: Hint — flamers may be required
- Crashed Ship Part: Hint — bring a strong squad
- Xenomorph Infestation: Hint — don't wait too long
- Power Cut: Xenomorphs cut colony power (event-specific)

## Requirements
- RimWorld 1.6
- No other mod dependencies required (HugsLib optional for additional logging)
